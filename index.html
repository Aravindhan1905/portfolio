About

Three crafts. One instinct for what holds attention.

I started out running ad campaigns and cutting videos to support them and somewhere along the way, stepping in front of the camera stopped feeling separate from the work behind it. Marketing taught me what makes people stop scrolling. Editing taught me how to hold that attention. Acting taught me how to earn it.

Today I move between all three building campaigns that are built to perform, edits that are built to be watched, and performances that are built to feel true. Whichever seat you need me in, the goal is the same: make something people actually remember.
